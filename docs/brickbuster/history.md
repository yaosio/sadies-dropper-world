# How Brickbuster was built

The steps it was built in. Never needed for a task; only read before undoing a choice.

It was built in five steps (the owner's plan, 2026-09-29), all done, and then reworked once:

1. **The room and the game (built).** The tall room, the case, the paddle and its faces, the
   controls, the cracks and the 90s sounds, and the heap of knocked-out bricks (`playing.md`).
2. **The break (built).** The shatter, the escape and the room left OUT OF ORDER
   (`the-break.md`).
3. **Out in the hall (built).** The yarn ball loose in the hall, and Sadie chasing it
   (`the-hall.md`).
4. **Sadie's sounds (built, 2026-09-30).** Her rare, soft sounds while she plays in the hall
   (`the-hall.md`).
5. **Arcade music (built, 2026-09-30).** The music while you play the machine (`playing.md`).
6. **Lives, levels and the machine mending itself (built, 2026-10-10).** The owner's playtester wanted
   to play more: three lives instead of cracks at the bottom, endless levels with a new board each,
   a high score, a wider glass, the ball kept exactly when you leave, and the machine mending itself
   after the break (`playing.md`, `boards.md`, `mending.md`). Before this, missing cracked the
   glass at the bottom (three cracks there broke it too, and so did clearing every brick), and it
   stayed broken until the pause menu started it over.
